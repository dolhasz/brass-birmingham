    What these numbers mean

    This dashboard aggregates public reporting. Every figure on it describes what is being published and how markets have moved — not verified conditions on the ground.

    Composite index

    A weighted blend of three observable components:

    • Theater reporting intensity (45%) — volume and recency of newswire items classified to each watch theater, weighted by that theater's standing risk baseline.
    • Market stress (30%) — same-day moves in VIX, gold, Brent crude, natural gas and the S&P 500.
    • Escalation language density (25%) — the share of the last 24 hours of wire items containing escalation-signal terms.

    Important limits

    • Reporting volume tracks media attention. It rises with news cycles and falls where journalists cannot operate — a quiet theater is not a safe one.
    • Theater levels are floored near their baseline so an ongoing war does not read as resolved on a slow news day.
    • Keyword classification produces occasional mistakes. Always read the linked source.
    • Nothing here is predictive. There is no model of intent, capability or escalation probability.
    • The seismic panel flags shallow events near known test sites as a geometry heuristic. Natural earthquakes and mining blasts trip the same conditions; USGS's own classification is authoritative.
